Det. 02

Load times cut across the board

Scenes took ten minutes to open — the loudest case, but not the only one. Profiling the actual cause rather than the assumed one, then rebuilding how assets load, brought scene opening under three minutes and shortened load times elsewhere in the pipeline with it. Three minutes is where the current phase landed, not where it stops.

Det. 03

From 2D plan to loaded 3D scene

A packer that lays plans out in CAD, paired with an importer that opens the result in 3D with every matching model already placed — manual oversight and variation control kept in the loop, because the point was to remove the file-hunting, not the judgement. It touches every project the 2D and 3D department runs.

Closing the gap between field and office

A mobile-friendly tool for the people on site to report in, with tracking and statistics at the office end. Replaces phone calls and loose photos with something the office can actually query and count.

General note

Details 02 and 03 compound. Shorter load times across the board, combined with an automated plan-to-scene handoff, take hours out of each project for a whole department of 2D and 3D staff. Over a year that adds up to days or weeks of recovered work time — an estimate from the floor, not a measured figure.
